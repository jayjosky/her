About us

A refugee-led organization building self-reliance in Kalobeyei

Help Empower Refugees (HER) was founded in 2024 by refugees and formally registered in 2026 in Turkana County, Kenya. We equip young people with education, practical skills and livelihood opportunities to build self-reliance, strengthen their families and drive positive change in their communities.

Our vision

A world where everyone is empowered to live a better, self-reliant life.

Our mission

To empower women, youth and families through education, skills development, entrepreneurship and sustainable livelihood opportunities that foster self-reliance and resilient communities.

What guides us
R

Refugee-led

Decisions are made by people who live the realities we work on.

Y

Youth-centered

Young people lead the programs, the farms and the cameras.

P

Practical

Skills that pay: poultry, agriculture, media and enterprise.

I

Inclusive

Refugees, asylum seekers and host communities, together.

Where we work

Kalobeyei & Kakuma, Turkana County

HER primarily serves refugees and asylum seekers in the Kalobeyei Integrated Settlement and Kakuma Refugee Camp, with selected programs also engaging surrounding host communities in Turkana County.

Leadership: James Ladu Sabastiano, Founder and Director.
