Our mission

Community-led solutions, built by the people who live here

HER was founded in 2024 by refugees responding to limited access to education, skills and employment among young people in Kalobeyei and Kakuma. We work with refugees, asylum seekers and the surrounding host communities — especially youth, women and children.

Our vision is a world where everyone is empowered to live a better, self-reliant life.

What we do

Practical programs that turn skills into income, and income into stronger families.

Livelihoods

Poultry & climate-smart agriculture

Youth-run poultry units — from brooding day-old chicks to market-ready birds — creating food and income.

Skills & enterprise

Digital skills

Photography, media production and computer literacy so young people can earn and tell their own stories.

Community

Community engagement

Outreach, food support and safe spaces that bring refugee and host community families together.

Wellbeing

ECD, education & SRHR

Early childhood development, community education and health awareness for children, youth and women.
